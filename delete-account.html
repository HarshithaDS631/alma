Account & Data Deletion Request

Alumni Network • RV Educational Institutions

Important Notice: Deleting your account will permanently and irreversibly erase your profile, credentials, messages, posts, resumes, and active connections. Academic degrees and university records held in official institutional registries will not be affected.

1. In-App Instant Deletion Option

If you currently have the mobile application installed, you can delete your account instantly:

  • Open the Alumni Network app.
  • Go to Profile > Settings (⚙️) > Login & Security.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm your choice and authenticate with your password.

2. Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, submit your details below to request permanent deletion by our data protection team:

Deletion Request Received

We have received your account deletion request. A confirmation email has been dispatched to verify your identity. Upon verification, your account and all associated personal data will be completely purged within 7 business days.

3. Data Deletion Timeline & Retention Standards

In accordance with GDPR, DPDPA 2023, and App Store guidelines:

  • Immediate Action: All active sessions, refresh tokens, and authentication cookies are invalidated immediately.
  • Profile & Content: Your profile, uploaded resumes, direct chats, and posts are permanently removed within 48 hours.
  • Audit Logs: Pseudonymized server logs are retained in encrypted storage for 180 days strictly for statutory cybersecurity forensic requirements (CERT-In).

4. Contact Us Directly

You can also request manual data erasure by emailing our Data Protection Officer directly: rvmediadevelopers@gmail.com
