### 2.1 Information You Choose to Give Us
When you interact with our Services, you directly provide us with information:
- **Account & Registration Information:** Your full name, personal/institutional email address, secure password, chosen institution (e.g., RVCE, RVITM, RVCA), department/branch, joining year, and graduation year.
- **Profile & Career Details:** Professional bio, current organization, job title, industry domain, years of experience, professional skills, LinkedIn profile URL, personal website, and uploaded profile photos.
- **Career Documents:** Resume files (PDF) uploaded to your profile for recruiter discovery and job applications.
- **Communications & Content:** Messages sent in direct chats, discussions, posts, comments, media attachments, event registrations, and feedback submitted through the App.
- **Authentication & Verification:** Email verification OTP codes, login credentials, and verification requests submitted to Institution Administrators.

### 2.2 Information We Get When You Use Our Services
When you use our App or web platform, we automatically collect technical and usage details:
- **Usage Activity:** Features you use, pages visited (e.g., Directory, Job Board, Feed, Events), search queries, and interaction timestamps.
- **Device & Technical Information:** Hardware model, operating system version, browser type, unique device identifiers, IP address, and network connection type.
- **Push Notification Tokens:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens used solely to deliver real-time notifications for messages, job listings, and institutional announcements.
- **Local Storage & Session Tokens:** Encrypted session JWT tokens and theme preferences stored securely on your device via AsyncStorage to keep you securely signed in.

### 2.3 Information We Get from Third Parties
If you choose to use social sign-in or third-party integrations:
- **Google / Apple / LinkedIn Authentication:** When you log in with Google, Apple, or LinkedIn OAuth, we receive your name, verified email address, and profile avatar URL provided by that service to streamline your registration. We do not receive your third-party account password.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for specific, legitimate institutional and networking purposes:

1. **Provide and Maintain the Services:** Authenticate your identity, set up your profile, facilitate alumni connections, deliver messaging, and display verified batch directories.
2. **Career & Mentorship Opportunities:** Match alumni with relevant job openings, internship postings, mentorship requests, and institutional projects.
3. **Safety, Integrity & Verification:** Verify alumni identity through Institution Administrator moderation, detect fraudulent accounts, prevent harassment, and enforce our Community Guidelines.
4. **Communications & Alerts:** Send transactional emails (e.g., OTP codes, password reset links, account approval status), security notices, and administrative updates.
5. **Analytics & Institutional Improvement:** Generate aggregated, anonymized reports on alumni employment trends, batch demographics, and platform engagement to improve educational programs and career services.

## 4. How We Share Information

We share your information only in the limited circumstances described below:

### 4.1 With Other Users of the Alumni Network
- **Public / Directory Profile:** Depending on your settings, verified members of your institution can view your name, branch, batch year, professional headline, skills, and bio in the alumni directory.
- **Direct Communications:** Messages, posts, and comments you publish are visible to the recipients or community members in those channels.
- **Resume Sharing:** Your uploaded resume is accessible only when you choose to apply for specific job postings or share it with institutional recruiters.

### 4.2 With Institution Administrators
- Designated administrators from your respective RV institution have access to verify registration applications, approve student/alumni status, moderate posted content, and manage institution-specific announcements.

### 4.3 With Trusted Service Providers
We partner with top-tier technology providers who process data strictly under our instructions and confidentiality agreements:
- **Database & Cloud Storage:** MongoDB Atlas (AWS/GCP hosted with enterprise encryption at rest and in transit)
- **Authentication & Push Notifications:** Google Firebase (FCM / Firebase Auth)
- **Transactional Email Delivery:** SendGrid (Twilio) / SMTP services for delivery of OTP and system emails
- **Web Application Hosting:** Vercel Inc.

### 4.4 For Legal Compliance & Safety
We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, governmental regulation, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or physical safety of our users, institutions, or the public.

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Information

We adhere to strict data minimization and retention timelines:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Action Upon Expiry / Account Deletion |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Active Profile Data** | Duration of active account | Kept while account is active |
| **Direct Messages & Posts** | Retained while active | Deleted when conversation/account is deleted |
| **Activity & Security Logs** | Up to **12 months** | Automatically purged / anonymized |
| **Deleted Accounts** | Up to **30 days** grace period | **Permanently purged** from production databases |
| **Encrypted Backups** | Up to **90 days** | Overwritten and permanently destroyed |

## 6. Control Over Your Information

You have robust, direct controls over your personal data:

- **Access & Review:** You can view all profile information directly in the App at any time via **Profile > Edit Profile**.
- **Correction & Updates:** You can edit your name, contact details, work experience, bio, and resume directly in-app.
- **Download / Data Portability:** You can request an export copy of your profile and data by emailing **rvmediadevelopers@gmail.com**.
- **Push Notification Controls:** You can enable or disable push notifications in your device settings.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** You can request permanent account deletion directly in the App under **Settings > Legal > Data Rights** or by submitting a written request to **rvmediadevelopers@gmail.com**. Upon confirmation, all personal identifiers, documents, and credentials will be permanently erased.

## 7. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical, organizational, and physical security measures:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All traffic is encrypted using **HTTPS/TLS 1.3**.
- **Encryption at Rest:** All databases and backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
- **Password Protection:** User passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are salted and hashed using **bcrypt** (cost factor 10+).
- **Access Controls:** Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) ensures only authorized administrators have access to administrative tools.
- **API Protection:** Rate limiting, parameter sanitization, and NoSQL injection defenses safeguard all backend services.
