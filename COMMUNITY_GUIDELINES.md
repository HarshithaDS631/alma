# Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** 7 August 2026  
**Alumni Network** — RV Educational Institutions

---

## Our Community Values

The Alumni Network app is a professional space for RV alumni, students, faculty, and staff. We are committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.

---

## 1. Be Respectful

- Treat all members with dignity regardless of background, gender, religion, or career stage.
- Constructive criticism is welcome. Personal attacks, insults, or harassment are not.
- Keep discussions professional, especially in public posts and mentorship conversations.

---

## 2. Be Authentic

- Use your real name and genuine professional information.
- Do not impersonate other alumni, faculty, students, or administrators.
- Do not create multiple accounts or fake profiles.

---

## 3. Content Standards

**Allowed:**
- Career updates, job postings, internship opportunities
- Educational discussions and academic advice
- Alumni achievements and success stories
- Events, announcements, and institutional news
- Mentorship offers and requests

**Not Allowed:**
- Spam, unsolicited commercial messages, or MLM promotions
- Political propaganda or divisive content
- Explicit, offensive, or sexually suggestive material
- Hate speech targeting any group or individual
- False news, misinformation, or rumours

---

## 4. Networking & Messaging

- Connect only with people you have a genuine interest in networking with.
- Do not send bulk unsolicited connection requests.
- Mentorship requests must be respectful and professional.
- Harassment of any kind in private messages will result in immediate suspension.

---

## 5. Reporting & Enforcement

- Use the in-app **Report** button to flag content or user behaviour that violates these guidelines.
- Reports are reviewed by institution administrators.
- Violations may result in:
  - Content removal
  - Account warning
  - Temporary suspension
  - Permanent account ban
