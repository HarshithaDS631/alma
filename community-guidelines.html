Community Guidelines

1. Professional Respect & Integrity

The Alumni Network is a dedicated community for alumni, students, and faculty. We foster a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment. Treat all members with dignity regardless of career stage, seniority, department, gender, or background.

2. Authentic Identity

  • Use your genuine legal name and accurate educational credentials.
  • Impersonation of batchmates, faculty members, institutional leadership, or recruiters is strictly prohibited and results in immediate account banning.
  • Do not register multiple accounts or automated bot scrapers.

3. Content Standards

Permitted Content:

  • Career milestones, promotion announcements, and job opportunities.
  • Academic research, project collaborations, and technological discussions.
  • Mentorship requests, professional guidance, and institutional announcements.
  • Alumni achievements, campus reunions, and educational events.

Strictly Prohibited:

  • Harassment, threats, stalking, hate speech, or defamatory attacks.
  • Spam, multi-level marketing (MLM), chain messages, or unapproved bulk sales.
  • Sexually explicit, pornographic, or violent imagery and text.
  • Deceptive job listings, fraudulent compensation claims, or academic misconduct.

4. Reporting and Moderation

  • Every post, comment, and message includes a direct Report button.
  • Users can immediately Block any member who engages in unwelcome communication.
  • Reports are reviewed by human institutional administrators within 24 hours.
  • Violations result in immediate content takedown, formal warnings, or permanent revocation of access.
