1. Introduction

RV Educational Institutions ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Alumni Network mobile application and web portal. This Privacy Policy details our principles, legal bases, and technical measures governing the collection, processing, protection, retention, and deletion of personal data across our services in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023 (India), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and mobile application store requirements.

2. Summary of Key Principles

  • No Data Brokering: We strictly never sell, trade, or monetize your personal data to advertisers or third-party brokers.
  • Purpose Limitation: Personal data is processed exclusively for institutional alumni networking, verified batch connections, mentorship pairing, and professional career enablement.
  • User Sovereignty: You retain complete ownership and rights to review, update, export, or permanently erase your personal data at any time.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS/HTTPS encryption, while database assets and backups are encrypted at rest with industry-standard AES protocols.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Registration & Identity: Full legal name, institutional/personal email address, batch graduation year, department/degree, and mobile phone number.
  • Professional Profile: Current company, job title, industry domain, professional skills, biography, LinkedIn profile link, and uploaded profile photos.
  • Career Assets: Resume documents (PDF format) uploaded voluntarily for recruiter discovery.
  • User-Generated Content: Articles, comments, discussions, event registrations, and direct messages sent through network communication channels.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Network Data: IP address, device OS and hardware model, browser characteristics, and network connection status.
  • Push Tokens: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device identifiers utilized exclusively for instant in-app alerts and notifications.
  • Security Audit Trails: Login timestamps, session duration, and multi-tenant access logs used for cyber defense and fraud prevention.

4. How We Use and Process Your Information

We process your data strictly under recognized legal bases (user consent, institutional verification, and legitimate interest):

  • To verify alumni credentials against institutional records before granting access.
  • To facilitate professional networking, directory search, and 1-to-1 mentorship engagements.
  • To send critical transactional notifications (OTP codes, password recovery, event reminders).
  • To prevent abuse, detect automated bots, and enforce our Community Guidelines and EULA.

6. Your Data Rights & Account Deletion

You have full legal rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access & Portability: View and export your personal information directly from your profile.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit any outdated educational or employment details anytime.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app (Profile > Settings > Login & Security > Delete Account) or via our dedicated web portal: Account Deletion Page.

7. Data Retention Policy

Personal data is retained only as long as your account remains active. Upon executing an account deletion request, authentication records, profiles, resumes, and direct messages are immediately wiped from primary databases. System activity logs are kept for a maximum of 180 days solely for regulatory cybersecurity compliance (CERT-In directives).
