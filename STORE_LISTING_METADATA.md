## 3. Data Safety & Privacy Declarations

### Data Collected:
1. **Name, Email Address, Phone Number (Optional)**: For account creation, authentication, and user profile.

5. **Account Deletion**: Users can permanently delete their account and all associated data directly in-app under Profile > Settings > Delete Account.
